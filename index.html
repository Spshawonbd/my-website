The Story of a Broken Boy

There was a boy named Rimon. He truly loved a girl and did everything to keep her happy. He gave his time, care, and even his savings for her. Rimon believed that true love never leaves.

One day, the girl slowly started changing. She stopped replying, avoided calls, and grew distant. After a few days, Rimon found out the truth— she was in a relationship with someone richer and more successful.

He focused on himself, his work, and his future. Now he knows— Heartbreak is not the end, it is the beginning of self-respect.

  • When you go to meet her, never forget three things:

  • and her favorite dress

Ten Years Later — A Cinematic Story

Night.
A lonely bridge.
Cold wind.

Rimon stood at the edge of the bridge, staring into the darkness. His heart was shattered. Love had betrayed him. Life felt meaningless.

One step forward… and everything could end.

Suddenly —

A strong hand pulled him back.

He turned around.

It was Jhankar Mahbub.

A calm face. Sharp eyes. The presence of someone who had seen failure—and conquered it.

Conversation

Jhankar Mahbub: “Stop. Why are you trying to end your life?”

Rimon (crying): “I was betrayed. The girl I loved destroyed me. She is happy with someone else. I can’t take this pain anymore. I don’t want to live.”

Jhankar Mahbub looked at him quietly… then smiled slightly.

Jhankar Mahbub: “Do you know what hurts more than failure? Giving up. Life doesn’t end when someone leaves. Life begins when you decide to fight back.”

He placed a hand on Rimon’s shoulder.

Jhankar Mahbub: “Come with me. Let me show you how broken people become powerful.”

That night, a life was saved.

The Mentor

Jhankar Mahbub was not just a man. He was a mentor, a motivational leader, and an author who wrote books about learning, failure, and growth.

He taught Rimon one simple rule:

Skill is power.

Final Lesson

Then why are you wasting time on this? Go and generate skills quickly.
